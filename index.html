PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE

Built to fail closed.

TESTNET HARDENEDIndependent audit pending
01

Pooled lending

Indexed supplier and borrower accounting with utilisation-driven rates and explicit reserve factors.

02

Isolated RWA vaults

Private-credit accounting is kept separate from permissionless pooled markets.

03

Bitkub Oracle

Prices fail closed on invalid, future or stale updates, with bounded feed configuration.

04

Interest rate model

Kinked utilisation curves price borrowing while supplier yield excludes protocol reserves.

05

Liquidations

Close factors, liquidation bonuses and collateral-limited repayment protect market solvency.

06

Bad debt

Accrued reserves absorb unrecoverable debt before any supplier loss is socialised.

07

KUB settlement

Transactions settle on KUB Testnet chain 25925 through MetaMask-compatible EIP-1193 wallets.

08

Security controls

Caps, two-step ownership, emergency pause, strict token accounting, Foundry testing and Slither analysis.

TESTNET SOFTWARE.Maddeth has not completed an independent production audit. Do not use this build with production funds or real-value RWA assets.
